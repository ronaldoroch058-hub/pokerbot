NOTES

HOW DO I READ THE TREE?

Start at the wall and go right. Each branch is one action by one seat, the percentage under it is how often hands took that branch, and circles are new cards. The busiest line runs straight across; the others drop off below it. Boxes are where hands ended: filled for a showdown, empty when everyone else folded.

IS IT REALLY LIVE?

Yes. Worker processes on the server play hands the whole time, and each finished hand is pushed to this page as it happens. Pause only stops your copy of the book; the bots keep playing and the hands queue up.

HOW DO THE BOTS DECIDE?

MONTE rolls out random boards for its equity and reads it off two power curves: the most it will call and the most it will bet. CFR-7 solved a small version of every street ahead of time by counterfactual regret minimisation and samples from that mix. NASH plays the textbook ratios: bluffs in proportion to its bet size, defends to minimum defence frequency, never adjusts to anyone.
